Bubble area = events per hour recorded, at the chosen thresholds. Fill = presence status from your verdicts: confirmed = at least one detection judged correct; judged wrong = every detection judged there was another sound (not proof of absence); not judged yet; not checked = Call is not in the presence check; none detected at this threshold. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, tap a site to open it. Base maps come from IGN (internet): IGN sees which tiles are shown, never the site positions. "Off" works offline.

Time from sunset

Events per time bin (5 min; 2 and 1 min when zoomed in). Recording starts at sunset (0) and lasts 2 h; time is read from the recorders' schedule, not their clock. "All sites" follows the département filter.

Season overview

Top: all sites pooled, events per hour recorded each week; below it, how many sites were recording. Bottom: for each site, the recording period (grey bar) and the first and last detection (dots). A hollow dot lies within 3 days of the start or end of recording: the season may extend beyond what was recorded. Follows the class, threshold and département filters.
